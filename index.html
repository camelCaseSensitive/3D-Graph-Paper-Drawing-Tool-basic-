Graph-Paper 3D Drawer

Projection rule: x = 2 graph squares right per unit, z = 2 graph squares up per unit, y = 1 graph square diagonally back per unit.

Drag the paper to pan. Use the mouse wheel / trackpad to zoom. On touch screens, drag with one finger. The geometry stays locked to the graph-paper proportions.

In the multi-box field, use x, y, z, or x, y, z @ ox, oy, oz to place a box at a 3D offset. Leave it blank to use the three number boxes above.
x → right · y ↖ back · z ↑ up
